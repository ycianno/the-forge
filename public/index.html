1. Click Generate above to create your sync token.

2. On your Mac, open Terminal in the Forge directory and run:

bash install-sync.sh

3. Paste your Forge URL and sync token when prompted.

4. A "The Forge" list will appear in Apple Reminders with today's quests. ✅

Sync runs every 5 minutes while your Mac is on. To stop: bash uninstall-sync.sh
